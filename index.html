WiFi TV Sender

Cast this computer's screen, a window or a browser tab to your phone over Wi-Fi.

Ready

Phone setup: open WiFi TV Receiver, type … into Laptop address, tap Start Receiving, then enter the code above.

Sharing audio (read this if the phone is silent)
  • Browser tab: pick the Chrome Tab / Edge Tab page in the picker and tick Also share tab audio. Works on Windows, macOS, Linux and ChromeOS.
  • Entire screen: tick Also share system audio. This is supported on Windows only; on macOS and Linux browsers cannot capture system audio from a screen or window share.
  • A single window: browsers do not offer audio for window shares.
  • The audio checkbox appears at the bottom-left of the browser's share dialog. If it is missing, the chosen source has no capturable audio.
